# Round 14: one frozen associative-recall transfer

Frozen before training or scientific evaluation, 2026-10-12. Question: does
Round 13's cross-event explanation survive outside a counting task, and does
it add explanatory value over a simple cumulative token-count energy?
No hyperparameter search, early stopping, seed replacement or outcome-based
compression choice is permitted. This is a finite task-transfer experiment.

## Task and relation to prior work

Associative recall requires retrieving a value bound to a key; see
[Zoology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927) and its
[official MQAR generator](https://github.com/HazyResearch/zoology/blob/main/zoology/data/multiquery_ar.py).
This protocol adapts that key/value retrieval idea to a fully generative
query/answer language. It is not a reproduction of Zoology's masked-loss MQAR
benchmark: vocabulary, query placement and objective differ. In particular,
queries here must be generated by the model, not provided by an evaluator.

Each fresh episode contains eight unique keys, each once in randomly ordered
key/value pairs. Key IDs are [0,1,3,4,5,6,7,8], value IDs 9..16, START=2.
Each key receives an independent uniformly sampled value, with replacement.
The 16-token table is the prefix. Following START, the data law alternates a
uniformly drawn key and its associated value. Every episode has a fresh table.
The correct first answer cannot be determined from token counts alone: swapping
values assigned to two keys preserves the prefix histogram but changes answers.
A targeted task check records that distinction; model qualification checks
first-answer accuracy on unseen tables, not just late repeated answers.

Train five seeds [11,23,37,51,71], one fixed recipe: two-layer, one-head
pre-LayerNorm causal decoder, width128, GELU MLP width512, learned absolute
positions, no dropout, full17-token output softmax. Train suffix length130,
batch64, 5,000 AdamW steps, lr0.001, warmup200, cosine floor0.05,
weight decay0.01, gradient clip1. Prefix labels are ignored; all suffix
positions, including random queries, enter cross-entropy. Context147 covers
every evaluation position. No forced query, answer or special-token mask is
used in generation. The earlier Round 6 failed 64-key recall seeds stay intact;
this smaller, wider recipe is a new predeclared realization.

## Qualification and controls

On 512 unseen fixed validation episodes require teacher-forced all-answer
top1 >=0.90 AND first-answer top1 >=0.90 for each seed. Full-cache P also
generates 32 unmasked paths on each of two evaluation prefixes; per-seed mean
query validity must be >=0.95 and sampled answer accuracy >=0.80. Invalid
queries and their answers remain in the denominator and in the full output
law. Five qualified models are required for a task-level interpretation.
All failed models and their measurements remain in aggregates; no reruns or
post hoc filtering. Failure means TASK_UNQUALIFIED, not mechanism falsification.

Use two fixed heldout 16-token tables, shared across seeds. T=129 consumes
START,Y1,...,Y128 and makes 64 thinning events. Original prefix and START
stay exact. Each eligible suffix pair becomes one weight2 record, using the
existing larger-token/lower-token endpoint selector and all layers' own KVs.
Balanced Block has32 positive then32 negative events; Alternating switches
each event. Both antipodal phases,16 Q paths/phase, paired common uniforms.
The token-ID selector aligns with key/value roles on valid pairs; this is a
fixed intervention, not a role-neutral or unique causal mediation test.
Logical masses/physical budgets match, not necessarily local error vector laws.
Report pooled local attention squared error; its <=1.25 max/min screen is
descriptive, and failure limits a temporal-order interpretation.

## Endpoints and explanatory baselines

Generate each Q's own full-softmax continuation, replay P on the same tokens,
and sum exact conditional KL(Q_t||P_t). This estimates the mean fixed-schedule
sequence KL, not mixture KL or exhaustive-tree KL. Save real token-bias squared
against the uncompressed same input history at every prefix, normalized by
logical mass squared; integrate it over time. Save Q task quality separately.

On the same Q histories compute every64 event derivative through the full
multilayer causal unroll. Reuse the exact Round 13 interpolation and Fisher
definitions. Signs are already in endpoint selectors. Amplitude1 only; no
fitted coefficient for Fisher. Save full and diagonal quadratic energies,
per-phase Grams, pathwise trajectories and original/physical/smooth parity.
The quantity is conditional P-logit Fisher averaged over Q1 histories. It is
not P-law sequence Fisher, a sequence-KL Taylor coefficient at zero, a heldout
prediction, certificate or unique mediator.

At T=17,33,65,129 average paths, then phases/prefixes within each training seed.
Five seeds are independent. At T129 require full relative error <=0.25 in
>=4/5 seeds per policy, and separately full closer than diagonal in >=4/5.
Report Block/Alternating ordering in >=4/5 as a distinct screen; no universal
non-histogram ordering is assumed. Report per-configuration mean/median/max
relative error to expose aggregate cancellation.

Scalar histogram baseline: for each heldout training seed, fit a nonnegative
no-intercept coefficient c=sum(H*KL)/sum(H^2) on the other four seeds' two
policy means. Predict that seed's risks using integrated histogram energy H.
Report its errors and ratio mismatch beside Fisher. This tests one scalar
count-energy explanation, not every possible histogram-dependent predictor.
Fisher still uses evaluated histories and is not made heldout by this baseline.
No new histogram-advantage threshold is chosen after seeing results.

TASK_UNQUALIFIED overrides transfer claims. Otherwise Fisher screen passage
supports finite transfer, and histogram comparisons indicate the strength of
the scalar alternative. Failure is retained; no tuned rescue or broader claim.
Round 9 certificate failures and Round 11-13 results remain unchanged.

# Historical theory snapshots

The three Markdown drafts here are byte-preserving copies of the user-supplied
Round 2, 3 and 4 L01 notes, predating the real-model pilot. Their original files
remain unchanged. `manifest.json` records their content hashes.

Read the current [theory status](../../THEORY_STATUS.md) first: it corrects
bibliography and qualifies space, normalization, depth and generation claims.
The snapshots are historical context, not the current scientific result or a
complete independently verified proof. Numerical examples mentioned in them
are not the 144-case experiment and were not reproduced for this release.
